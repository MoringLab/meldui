# Community conduct

Treat contributors with respect. Discuss code and evidence rather than personal attributes. Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, unwanted contact and publication of private information are unacceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, limit participation or remove access to protect contributors. Enforcement should be proportionate and explain the behavior at issue. Report security concerns through SECURITY.md. A private conduct reporting contact must be added by the repository owner before public launch; do not post private reports in public issues.
